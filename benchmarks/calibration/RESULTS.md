# Held-out calibrated comparisons

Generated by `examples/paper/report_calibration.py`. Calibration seeds 100–102; evaluation seeds 0–5. All four methods receive identical selected positions, both shear-noise components, and weights. The target is unweighted galaxy-position, DC-removed relative L2 on truth κ<1. Means ± standard errors describe six catalogues; they are not confidence bounds on universal method performance.

The former large Argyris density advantage is not supported by this corrected comparison. The FEM arms are close; Argyris has a small paired advantage over P3 at baseline densities 20 and 30.
